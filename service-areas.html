The coverage rule, in full

Coverage is the first three characters of your postal code.

A Canadian postal code starts with three characters called a forward sortation area — the T5K in T5K 1A1. That is the part that decides coverage here. Every code below is accepted by the request form. Anything outside that set — T4N (Red Deer) or T6Y, both of which this form has been tested against and refuses — is turned away at the postal field, with the reason, before anything else is filled in.

What this page is not

No contractor names, no prices, no reviews.

  • There is no contractor named on this site, and none is rated, ranked or reviewed here. We do not take a cut of your repair bill.
  • There is no price on this site, because we do not do the work and we do not quote for it. A licensed contractor quotes you.
  • There is no map of "recent jobs" and no count of homes served, because those would have to be invented. The only numbers on this site are counts taken from its own form.

We are not connected to any gas utility or government body, and we do not speak for one. Check any contractor’s Alberta licence before you sign — a licensed contractor will hand it over without blinking.

What happens after you send this

  1. You send the request

    Name, phone, postal code and what the furnace is doing. That is the whole form, and it is on the request page.

  2. It goes to heating contractors who work in your part of Edmonton

    The postal code you gave decides which side of the city that is. Not a national call centre, not a blast to fifteen companies.

  3. You get called with a price

    Expect a call or a text. Ask for the price in writing and check whether permits and the old furnace removal are in it. Then decide — you owe nobody anything for the quote.
