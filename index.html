Who receives your request

One person, and then the contractors he sends it to.

Your request goes to Max Hemmerich, who runs this service in Edmonton. He reads it and passes it to heating contractors who do that work in your area. Nothing about you is published on this site, sold, or added to a list you did not ask for.

Those contractors receive your name, phone number, email if you left one, postal code and the job description. They receive it to quote the job. That is the only thing it is used for.

Want it deleted, or want to know what is held? Write to maxhemmerich@gmail.com and it is removed.

What we are not

Not a contractor. Not a utility. Not a directory.

  • We do not install, repair or service furnaces, and we do not quote for them. Licensed contractors do that.
  • We are not connected to any gas utility or government body, and we do not speak for one.
  • We do not rank contractors or take a cut of your repair bill. Contractors pay a flat price if they choose to receive requests — that is the whole business.

Check any contractor's Alberta licence and their standing before you sign anything. Ask for it — a licensed contractor will hand it over without blinking.
