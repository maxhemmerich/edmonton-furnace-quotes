The qualification rule

A request is billable only when all four of these are true.

  1. The postal code is in your coverage area

    Edmonton, or one of the metro codes listed on this page. A request from Red Deer is not a lead for you and is not billed.

  2. The job is one you actually do

    Furnace replacement, no-heat, or a major furnace repair. Tune-ups, thermostat swaps and AC-only calls are filtered out before they reach you — if one slips through, it is free.

  3. The homeowner engages

    They answer your call or reply to you within 48 hours of you receiving it. A number that rings out for two days is not a lead and is not billed.

  4. You claimed it inside the window

    Six hours on a shared request, or it is yours alone on the retainer. No claim, no charge.

How you are billed

One page, once a month, by e-transfer.

We send a list of the month's leads with the date, postal code and job type, and the e-transfer address. You pay for the qualified ones. Before that, you have the leads — we are not holding delivery until a payment clears.

If it does not meet the rule, you do not pay for it. That is the whole dispute process. Tell us a lead was junk and it comes off the invoice, no argument.

Month one, first three qualified leads are free regardless of which plan you are on. Nothing to cancel, nothing to sign.
